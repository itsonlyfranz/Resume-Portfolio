FinSight Insight LLM fine-tunes a small open model to write the spending insights, simulation summaries and goal messages that the FinSight iOS app produces from its own facts. The aim is a model small enough to run on-device that follows the app's output contract every time and never changes a money figure. All results are from a single run on a synthetic held-out test set of 246 examples, so they show the approach rather than a production guarantee.

## Results

Held-out test set, n=246 (117 insight, 78 simulation, 51 goal), greedy decoding, Mac mini M2 Pro with 32 GB.

| Model | Number fidelity | Format valid | ROUGE-L |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base, zero-shot | 97.6% | 58.9% | 0.252 |
| Base + 2-shot prompt | 100% | 95.1% | 0.373 |
| Fine-tuned (LoRA) | 100% | 100% | 0.454 |
| Fine-tuned, GGUF Q5_K_M | 100% | 100% | 0.456 |

## Quantization

- **Q5_K_M** is the winner at 1.1 GB, 70.9 tok/s and 0.92 s mean latency, with no loss in number fidelity or format validity.
- **Q8_0** is 1.6 GB at 71.8 tok/s, so it costs more bytes for little speed gain.
- **Q4_K_M** is the smallest and fastest at 0.99 GB and 83.5 tok/s, but it is the only quantization that breaks the output format, at 98.0% valid.

## Training

- **Base model** is Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, fine-tuned with LoRA rank 16 through MLX.
- **Schedule** is 600 iterations in 1,790 s, with a peak memory footprint of 14.55 GB.
- **Dataset** is 1,618 train, 114 validation and 246 test synthetic examples.
- **Export** converts the merged model to GGUF with llama.cpp and publishes the weights on Hugging Face.

## Evaluation

- **Number fidelity** is the pass rate of a port of the app's money-figure guard, so a changed figure counts as a failure.
- **Format valid** checks that the raw output follows the app's output contract.
- **ROUGE-L** is scored against templated references, so it measures closeness to a house style rather than human preference.
- **Fact coverage** is the share of reference money figures the output states.
