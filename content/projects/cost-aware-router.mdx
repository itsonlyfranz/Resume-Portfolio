Cost-Aware Agent Router is built on the premise that most agent traffic doesn't need the most expensive model: it routes each task to the cheapest model that can handle it, stops as soon as the answer is confident, and caps spend per task. The 43.7% figure comes from the bundled demo with simulated models, so it illustrates the mechanism rather than a production saving.

## Architecture

- **agent.py** provides `CostAwareRouter`, the LangGraph `StateGraph` over a typed `RouterState` with an optional checkpointer and a recursion limit.
- **plan_call** is the budget gate that picks the wanted tier, or the nearest cheaper tier that fits the remaining token and USD budget.
- **evaluate** is the early-exit decision: confidence at or above the threshold exits, otherwise it escalates one tier, and at premium the answer is accepted.
- **finalize** assembles the `Decision` and appends it to the JSONL log.
- **complexity.py**, **budget.py** and **confidence.py** hold the complexity score, `TokenBudget`, and the structured confidence prompt and parser.
- **analytics.py** keeps the local JSONL `DecisionStore` with `summarize()` and `render()`, and **client.py** defines the `LLMClient` protocol with `AnthropicClient` and an offline `FakeClient`.
