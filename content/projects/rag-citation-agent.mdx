RAG Agent with Citation Grounding treats the model's output as untrusted: every claim is verified against the retrieved text before it reaches the user, and a claim that cannot be traced to a verbatim quote in a real source is removed. The answer is then scored, flagged, and, if weak, retried against search results.

## Architecture

- **agent.py** is the LangGraph `StateGraph` with typed `AgentState` and nodes `retrieve`, `answer_from_kb`, `weak_retrieval`, `search`, `answer_from_search` and `finalize`, plus an optional checkpointer.
- **index.py** handles chunking and a TF-IDF inverted index in a JSON-persisted `KnowledgeBase`.
- **generation.py** builds the prompt, with sources escaped and declared untrusted, and parses the model's JSON strictly.
- **grounding.py** is the claim verifier, checking that the source exists, the quote is verbatim, claim terms are covered, and every number appears in the quote.
- **confidence.py** implements the scoring formula, and **search.py** defines the `SearchProvider` protocol with a local `SnapshotSearch`.
- **llm.py** and **offline.py** keep the model behind a one-method `LLMClient` protocol, with `AnthropicClient` for Claude and offline clients for keyless runs.
