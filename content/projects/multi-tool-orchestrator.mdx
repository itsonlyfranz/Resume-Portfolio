Multi-Tool Orchestrator is a LangGraph agent for coordinating complex workflows: it plans a goal as a capability DAG, routes each step to the best permitted tool from a live registry, runs independent steps in parallel, and reconciles conflicts between tools.

## Architecture

- **Agent graph (graph.py)** is the `StateGraph` with typed `RunState` and nodes `plan`, `review`, `schedule`, `run_step` and `synthesize`, using conditional edges, `Send` fan-out and `interrupt`.
- **Orchestrator (orchestrator.py)** is the facade that builds the graph with a `MemorySaver` or SQLite checkpointer and exposes `run`, `execute_plan` and `resume`.
- **Planner (planner.py)** asks the LLM for a JSON plan over a permission-filtered capability catalog and validates it as a DAG with known capabilities.
- **Tool registry (registry.py)** and **Router (router.py)** index tools by capability, then filter and rank providers with `balanced`, `cheapest`, `fastest` and `most_reliable` strategies.
- **Permission policy (permissions.py)**, **Resource manager (resources.py)** and **Conflict resolver (conflicts.py)** handle scoped access, read/write claims, and reconciling disagreeing results.
- **Step runner (step_runner.py)** and **Audit log (audit.py)** execute one step with retry and fallback, and record routing, denials, fallbacks and conflicts.
